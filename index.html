PS2 Save Toolkit

Manage PS2 memory card images in your browser. Import and export saves, copy saves between cards, and view original 3D icons.

Open standard 8 MiB card images, including .ps2, .mc2 and .bin. Import PSU, MAX, CBS, SPS, XPS and signed PS2 PSV packages, directly or from ZIP and RAR archives. Export PSU, MAX or signed PS2 PSV.

Files stay in your browser. Original inputs stay unchanged. Download your card before closing or refreshing the toolkit.

Read the PS2 save guides for instructions, supported formats and limits.
